A Global Initiative for Kingdom Impact

Our Roots

Since 2015, SUG & partners have been hosting community competitions, entrepreneurship training, workshops and motivational events across communities in Africa, UK & USA.

The Collaboration

Leveraging these experiences, Godly.id have teamed up with SUG & Its Partners to host the 1st round of the Community Transformation Through Acts of Love, Evangelism & Enterprise, dedicated to encouraging entrepreneurs, sociopreneurs, missionaries, intercessor's, evangelist, street pastors and community loving purpose driven individuals to birth creative scalable bite sized initiatives which share God's love and bring lasting transformation by either winning souls, solving a problem in their community and or ignite lasting positive change.

Our Heart

"Our dedication is to support the exploration of how through an idea to 'Show Love' to the community, participants can pilot projects or ventures to reality in ways that births transformation."

The Initiative

This initiative is designed to steer up and promote the concepts of creative evangelism and the sharing of God's love in ways that bring lasting transformation to communities in need across the nations by empowering purpose-driven individuals to launch scalable bite sized projects and ventures that demonstrate God's love, win souls, and bring lasting change to communities in need. We exist to help the igniting of creative problem solving juices and the turning of simple ideas into real, impactful pilot projects.

Global Invitation

We are particularly keen to receive applications in all categories from participants in Africa, Asia and South America, and warmly welcome creative evangelism applications from Europe and North America.

Our Core Objectives

Promote Innovative Evangelism

Demonstrating God's love through tangible solutions that bring transformation.

Empower Entrepreneurs

Support creative out of the box problem solving solutions and innovators building projects aligned with Kingdom values.

Increase Education

Equip participants with evangelism, project management and entrepreneurship facilitating projects and evangelism activities.

Grow Digital Engagement

Share inspiring stories of love, faith, and impact across digital platforms.
